## Direction contract

THESIS: A longevity clinic told as one continuous camera move: the visitor walks in, is scanned, and the camera enters the body to the cell. Refuses the blue-and-white hospital template and the neon sci-fi rut.
OWN-WORLD: Pearl and warm ivory grounds, champagne gold as the only accent, platinum for secondary ink, charcoal chapters where the frames turn dark and microscopic, very light blue only on scientific annotations. Headlines in light high-contrast capitals, body in a clean sans. The supplied frames are the imagery; gold dust particles in WebGL sit above them.
STORY: Visitor sees a calm luxury clinic, follows a patient through consultation and a scan, goes inside the body to blood, DNA and mitochondria, learns what regenerative science does and does not claim, and requests a private conversation.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Frame one fills the screen (patient entering the clinic). Headline INSIDE THE FUTURE OF HUMAN LONGEVITY left, one sentence under it, one button EXPLORE THE SCIENCE, minimal nav with wordmark, a quiet scroll indicator.
FORM: brief-pinned direction (user supplied structure, palette, headlines, assets) beat the concept roll. seed: none (unavailable)
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
