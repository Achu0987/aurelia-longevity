# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

User-specified: Next.js, React, JavaScript only (no TypeScript), Three.js, GSAP and ScrollTrigger, HTML Canvas for the image sequence, Lucide React icons only when needed. Homepage only.

## Users

International, affluent adults (early 40s to 70s) considering a private longevity clinic, and the agency client who must be impressed at first sight. Inferred from the brief, not interviewed.

## Product Purpose

Aurelia (placeholder brand for a luxury longevity and regenerative medicine clinic): advanced diagnostics, cellular health, regenerative medicine and long-term health optimisation. The site must feel like a luxury medical brand crossed with a scientific research experience, never a hospital template. Success: the client says "this is a completely different level of healthcare website".

## Positioning

A single cinematic journey from the clinic door, through a full-body scan, into the body and its cells. The story is told with the supplied video frame sequence.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The primary visual asset is the user's own extracted frame sequence (600 frames, 240 unique, 1920x1080), a generated video. No replacement frames, no stock imagery.
- No medical promises: never claim treatments reverse ageing or guarantee results.
- Clinic name, address, team, prices and contact details are placeholders.
- Concept site; clinical details are illustrative.

## Brand Commitments

Palette named by the user: pearl white, warm ivory, champagne gold, platinum silver, soft metallic gradients, charcoal contrast sections, very light blue scientific accents. Editorial headings with clean sans body. Headlines supplied in capitals.

## Evidence on Hand

The frame sequence in Frames/video_frames (copied to public/frames). No photography, logos, testimonials, statistics or case studies. None may be invented.

## Product Principles

1. Scientific credibility and restraint before spectacle.
2. The frame sequence is the story; 3D supports it where it adds meaning.
3. Honest language about medicine: understanding, not promises.
4. Smooth, readable, accessible and fast on every device.
